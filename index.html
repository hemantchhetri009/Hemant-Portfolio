About

I'm in the second year of my BCA at SRM University in Chennai, and I'm from Nepal.

I like making things people can actually use: a civic reporting app, a real-time chat, a café dashboard. Cybersecurity is what I study hardest, and I write my own tools so I understand how they work. Video editing taught me about pacing, and it shows in how I build interfaces.

I'm open to freelance work and internships.
